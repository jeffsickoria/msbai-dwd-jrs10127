Weather model and holdout results

The original project modeled daily ridership using temperature, precipitation, snow, day of week, and month. On the final 179 days of model coverage, the overall mean absolute error was 18.0% lower than the seasonal-naive benchmark. The model underpredicted part of the recent growth period.
